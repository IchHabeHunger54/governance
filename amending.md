# Article 2: Amending Governance

## § 1 General

a. Changes to the Governance may be implemented by creating a pull request to the Governance repository.

b. Past versions of the Governance shall be available to the Members at all times.

> This is achieved by properly using Git. Essentially, this means no force-pushes to this repository, ever.

## § 2 Major Changes

a. A thread in an internal Discord channel shall be created to discuss the proposed changes. All Team Members shall be notified of the creation of the thread.

b. Comments on the wording of the pull request may be given on the pull request directly.

c. Once discussion has ended, a Supermajority Vote about the proposed changes among all Team Members shall be held. All Team Members shall be notified of the initiation of the Vote.

d. If the Supermajority Vote in point d succeeds, a member of the Steering Council shall merge the pull request. Otherwise, they or the original pull request opener shall close the pull request. All Team Members shall be notified of the result of the Supermajority Vote.

## § 3 Minor Changes

a. If a proposed change to the Governance is minor enough, a member of the Steering Council may merge a pull request immediately, without going through the thread and voting procedure outlined in § 2. A minor change is constituted by being a minor typographical, grammatical, structural or similarly small change; any changes to the meaning of the Governance shall go through § 2 instead.

> The intention of this clause is to allow fixes of typos or similar without notifying the entire team three whole times.

b. A member of the Steering Council merging a pull request immediately (as outlined in point a) shall send a message about the merge in a Team space, however they are not required to notify all team members.

c. Any team member may call for a takeback of an immediate merge (as outlined in point a) for any reason. In that case, the merge is to be reverted, and the regular procedure as outlined in § 2 is to be executed.
