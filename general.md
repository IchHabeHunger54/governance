# Article 1: General

## § 1 The Project

a. The NeoForged Project is a group of volunteers that maintain NeoForge and associated projects.

b. The NeoForged Project is comprised of the Hosted Services and the Community Spaces, and all Members therein.

c. The NeoForged Project is governed by the Governance, which is to be always available to all Members.

## § 2 What the Governance Is

a. The Governance is a non-exhaustive list of the working principles of the Project.

b. The Governance is intended to help guide the Project in times of uncertainty.

c. The Governance is guided by the principle of "people over processes". This means that by design, only the most necessary processes are outlined in the Governance.

d. The Governance is applicable to all Members. Nobody is above it.

> This rule is a direct consequence of the founding history of the Project, which separated itself from the Minecraft Forge project over this issue.

## § 3 What the Governance Is Not

a. The Governance is not intended to catch all possibilities, eventualities and edge cases; in other words, it is not exhaustive.

b. The Governance is not intended to be enforced or referenced on a day-to-day basis.

c. The Governance is not intended to prevent what is known as "rules lawyering".

> Rules lawyering is still very much not appreciated, it is just not explicitly forbidden, as it is hard to codify.

d. The Governance is not immutable. Its contents may be changed in accordance with [Art 2][art2].

## § 4 Goals and Ideals

a. The Project's main goal is to maintain NeoForge and the Hosted Services.

b. The Project aims to make NeoForge the best it can be, in matters of features, compatibility and performance.

c. The Project aims to welcome everyone who is interested to interact with the Project, the Team or the Community.

d. The Project holds itself and all Members to the [Contributor Covenant][contributorcovenant].

e. The Project holds itself and all Members to its Rules of Community Spaces, defined in § 5.

> Points d and e have significant overlap by design.

f. All Members are here for fun.

## § 5 Community Spaces

### Section 1: Rules of Community Spaces

a. Members shall treat each other with respect, in accordance with § 4d.

b. Members shall not send content that serves spam or advertising purposes.

c. Members shall not send content commonly considered "not safe for work", that is, depictions of violence against people or animals, sexually explicit content, or strongly sexually suggestive content.

d. Members shall not break the law, or endorse or teach to break the law. This extends especially to illegally obtained ("pirated") versions of Minecraft.

e. Members shall not endorse, teach how to make or use, or share hacks or cheats. Hacks and cheats are defined as anything intended and designed to provide unfair advantage in multiplayer scenarios.

f. Members shall use the various channels for their intended use.

### Section 2: Implementation of Section 1

a. The Team shall make a copy of Section 1 available to all Members in the Discord. The copy need not be verbatim, and may contain longer explanations if deemed necessary by the Team, so long as the spirit of Section 1 is kept intact.

b. Members shall report violations of Section 1 via a means provided to them by the Team, or by Discord/GitHub, as appropriate.

> At the time of writing, this is handled via the Mod Mail bot on Discord, and via the built-in reporting tools on GitHub.

## § 6 Enforcement

a. If needed, the Governance shall be enforced by the Moderators team (as defined by [Art 3][art3]) or the Steering Council, depending on the situation that requires enforcement.

b. The Moderators team shall enforce § 5.1 on Community Members.

c. The Steering Council shall enforce the Governance in all other situations, including enforcement of § 5.1 on Team Members.

## § 7 Compensation

a. No Member is entitled to financial or material compensation for their contributions to the Project.

b. The Project may set up a means for Members to donate money to the Project.

> At the time of writing, this means lives at https://opencollective.com/neoforged.

c. The Team may vote to give away parts of the donated money from point b to one or multiple Members whose contributions to the Project they deem worthy of compensation, in accordance with [Art 5][art5]. Team Members who would receive compensation may not participate in or initiate the vote.
