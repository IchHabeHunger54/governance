# Article 5: Voting

## § 1 General

a. A Team Member may call for a vote among the Team or a subteam they are on (as defined in [Art 3 § 2][art3§2]) at any time, for any reason.

> It is suggested that the "at any time, for any reason" be used sparingly and only as needed.

b. Votes are always required to give away donation money to Members ([Art 1 § 7.1c][art1§7.1]), put out a bounty ([Art 1 § 7.2][art1§7.2]), or accept an application or appointment of a new Team Member ([Art 3 § 3][art3§3]).

c. A vote takes the form of a Discord poll. Depending on the question, giving multiple answers may be enabled or disabled. All eligible Team Members shall be notified of the poll.

d. Any context for the poll shall be sent in accompanying messages, in as close proximity to the poll as possible.

e. The duration of the poll shall be announced alongside it. It shall be at least 72 hours, but may be longer if deemed appropriate by the poster. If the vote is time-sensitive, this may be included in the context and the time be shortened down, however it must still run for at least 24 hours.

> 72 hours, or 3 days, has been a good time for polls in the past, as it allows at least a full day for people to respond even when including timezones.

f. Eligible Team Members may request for the voting time to be extended. This request shall be accepted or rejected by the Team Member that originally called for the vote.

g. If appropriate, a discussion thread for the poll shall be created. Any discussion about the poll should be contained to that thread.

h. Simple Yes/No polls that run for more than 24 hours remaining, have a simple majority of support for the Yes option and no opposing votes cast may be fast-tracked by any Team Member by notifying the other eligible Team Members and announcing the fast-tracking. In that case, the vote ends 24 hours after the fast-tracking, unless an eligible Team Member votes No or requests the full time be used regardless.

i. A poll may be held in a public channel to additionally gauge the opinion of Community Members. For results, however, only the votes of Team Members shall be considered.

j. The decision made by a vote shall not be revisited for the 90 days after the end of the poll.

## § 2 Results

a. If the poll was a simple Yes/No poll, the poll is accepted if more than half the votes cast were Yes, and rejected if less than half the votes cast were No.

b. If the poll was a simple Yes/No poll and there was an exact 50/50 split of votes cast, or if there was a different mode of voting and multiple winning options with an equal amount of votes, the Steering Council's votes will be counted double to tie-break. If this does still not yield a conclusive outcome, the Steering Council shall hold a separate vote among themselves to tie-break, or if possible, come up with a middle-ground solution.

> For example, in the past, there was a vote on the naming scheme of `Interface` vs. `IInterface`. The vote came out 50/50. The Steering Council subsequently decided to not enforce either style, and both styles were allowed in the codebase.

c. If the poll was a multi-option poll that requires a singular winner, and no option received more than half the votes, the two options with the most votes shall be put to a run-off vote. If the second place is tied, all the tied options will participate in the run-off vote. If that process does not eliminate any options, or if the run-off vote does not change the outcome, the vote is considered rejected.

d. If the vote seeks to overturn a previous vote, all requirements for accepting the vote are raised to at least two thirds of the votes.

## § 3 Supermajority Votes

a. A supermajority vote is required for amending Governance (see [Art 2 § 2c-d][art2§2]) and for removing a Team Member by vote (see [Art 3 § 4][art3§4]).

b. Supermajority votes must always be Yes/No polls.

c. Supermajority votes must have at least two thirds of the votes in approval, and are considered failed otherwise.

d. Supermajority votes must have at least half of the team members cast a vote, and are considered failed otherwise.

e. Supermajority votes cannot be shortened to less than 72 hours, as otherwise allowed by § 1e.

f. Supermajority votes cannot be fast-tracked, as otherwise allowed by § 1h.
