# Governance of the NeoForged Project

## Terminology

- The **Project** refers to the NeoForged Project as defined in [Article 1 § 1][art1§1].
- The **Team**, if not qualified further, refers to the NeoForged team as defined in [Article 3][art3].
- The **Steering Council** refers to the Steering Council as defined in [Article 4][art4].
- A **Team Member** is a user that is considered a member of the Team.
- A **Community Member** is a user in a Community Space that is not also a Team Member.
- The **Community** is the entirety of all Community Members.
- A **Member** is either a Team Member or a Community Member.
- The **Community Spaces** refers to all spaces in which Team Members and Community Spaces can interact with each other. This includes the Discord and the GitHub.
- **Hosted Services** are electronic services, available to the Team and/or the Community, hosted by the Project.
- (The) **Discord**, or the **Discord Server**, refers to the NeoForged Discord server at https://discord.neoforged.net/.
- (The) **GitHub** refers to the NeoForged GitHub account at https://github.com/neoforged, or all repositories hosted under it, as determined by context.
- **NeoForge** is the main product of the Project that is made available to the general public.
- (The) **Governance** refers to this repository, or its contents, as determined by context.
- A **Vote** is a vote as defined in [Article 5][art5].
- A **Supermajority Vote** is a Vote with special requirements as defined in [Article 5][art5].
