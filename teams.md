# Article 3: Teams

## § 1 The NeoForged Team

a. The NeoForged Team consists of all users who are on at least one subteam, as defined in § 2.

> The initial members of the NeoForged Team were the ones who separated from the Minecraft Forge project. Huzzah, we solved the bootstrap paradox!

b. In Votes or other decisions that affect the entire Team, every Team Member gets exactly one vote, regardless of how many subteams they are on.

c. Team Members have a role model position among the Members, and should act accordingly.

d. The Team should have a channel readable by all Members but only writable by Team Members, and a channel readable only by Team Members to discuss sensitive topics.

> It is encouraged that as much discussion as possible happen in the public channel - #project-talk at the time of writing -, and as little as possible happen in the internal channels - #internal-private at the time of writing.

e. Subteams may request a channel for their team be created by the Steering Council. The channel should be public if possible, but may be private if the topics discussed are sensitive.

> For example, at the time of writing, the Docs Team has a public channel (#docs-talk), the Infrastructure Team has a private channel (#infrastructure-private), and the Bots Team does not have a dedicated channel.

f. New team members may join the team by joining a subteam. They may join a subteam by either applying, as outlined in § 3.1, or by being appointed, as outlined in § 3.2. Certain side teams may be limited to appointment only, making application impossible, as outlined in § 2.3c.

g. Every team shall receive a corresponding role on the Discord. If deemed necessary, a team shall also receive a corresponding role on GitHub.

## § 2 Subteams

### Section 1: Maintainers

a. Maintainers are responsible for maintaining and developing NeoForge and associated projects.

b. Maintainers are not responsible for maintaining and developing side projects that have a designated side team, as outlined in Section 3b.

c. Maintainers have writing permissions on all of the Project's GitHub repositories. They are advised to exercise these permissions responsibly and with caution.

d. Maintainers are explicitly not responsible to perform the duties of a Moderator (as defined in Section 2), unless they are simultaneously a Moderator as well.

### Section 2: Moderators

a. Moderators are responsible for enforcing the rules of Community Spaces, as outlined in [Art 1 § 5.1][art1§5.1].

b. Moderators may apply sanctions to Community Members that break the rules, by means of warning, muting, kicking, banning or similar options.

c. Moderators may not enforce the rules on other Team Members, as outlined in [Art 1 § 6c][art1§6c].

d. Moderators are explicitly not responsible to perform the duties of a Maintainer (as defined in Section 1), unless they are simultaneously a Maintainer as well.

### Section 3: Side Teams

a. Side teams are teams besides the Maintainers and Moderators. They are instated by the Steering Council or via Vote on demand.

b. Side teams may have their own repositories that they maintain and/or develop, removing them from the responsibility of the maintainers as defined in Section 1a-b.

c. Side teams may limit new members to appointment (as defined in § 3.1) only.

> For example, at the time of writing, the Infrastructure Team is limited to appointment only, while the Docs Team is not.

## § 3 Admission to Teams

### Section 1: Application

a. The Team shall provide a means for Community Members to apply to join any subteam, unless that team is limited to appointment only as outlined in § 2.3c.

b. The Team shall hold a Vote on whether to accept or reject the applicant.

> This is a vote amongst the whole team, as new Team Members also get all the permissions of the greater NeoForged Team, such as the right to vote in Steering Council elections.

c. If the vote defined in point b is successful, the new Team Member shall be supplied with their respective team's permissions and roles. Otherwise, a rejection notice shall be sent to them.

### Section 2: Appointment

a. Any Team Member may suggest appointing a Member to a subteam. The team shall hold a Vote on whether to accept or reject the applicant.

> This is a vote amongst the whole team, as new Team Members also get all the permissions of the greater NeoForged Team, such as the right to vote in Steering Council elections.

b. If possible and the Member being voted on is a Community Member, the appointment should be kept a secret to them.

c. If the vote defined in point a is successful, the new Team Member shall be supplied with their respective team's permissions and roles. Otherwise, a rejection notice shall be sent to them only if they have been previously informed, that is, if point b was not applicable.
